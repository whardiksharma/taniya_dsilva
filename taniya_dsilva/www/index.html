[ APPROACH & PHILOSOPHY ]

Taniya works at the intersection of research, strategy, and implementation. Social sector leaders frequently face the dual challenge of stewarding big structural ambitions while rallying ground teams to deliver under complex, resource-constrained conditions.

Her approach combines strategic foresight with institutional stamina: clarifying choices, conducting rigorous qualitative inquiry, aligning disparate donors and government bodies, and establishing delivery systems that endure long after initial pilots conclude.
