[ BIOGRAPHY & PHILOSOPHY ]

Bridging high-level strategy with ground-level reality.

15 years working at the nexus of research, strategy, and execution across LMIC contexts.

Taniya D’silva is a strategy and programme leader with 15 years of experience driving organisational strategy, multi-stakeholder programmes, executive communications, and donor and government partnerships across climate, gender equity, labour markets and public health in LMIC contexts.

She works at the intersection of research, strategy and implementation, helping organisations translate evidence and strategic priorities into practical programmes, partnerships and organisational action. Her experience spans advisory work with nonprofits, foundations, private sector, government bodies and other social-impact organisations.

Her approach combines strategic thinking with practical execution: understanding the evidence, clarifying the strategic choices, aligning stakeholders and translating priorities into implementable programmes and systems that endure.

Practice Positioning Note

Taniya maintains her primary digital identity as an independent strategic advisor, researcher, and board member. Her consulting vehicle, Wise Impact Consulting Solutions, operates alongside her independent advisory mandates, enabling bespoke consortia and programmatic implementation.

[ VALUES & HOW I WORK ]

Guiding Principles of Advisory Practice

01 · EVIDENCE

Evidence-Led Rigour

Every strategic recommendation is anchored in credible qualitative and quantitative data, not consulting assumptions or superficial trends.

02 · EXECUTION

Implementation Stamina

A strategy is only as viable as the team tasked with executing it. We design frameworks that withstand ground-level operational pressures.

03 · ALIGNMENT

Multi-Stakeholder Empathy

Bridging the vocabularies of corporate donors, community leaders, and government ministries to build durable mutual trust.

04 · CLARITY

Strategic Parsimony

Rejecting corporate jargon and over-engineered decks. Clear choices, decisive roadmaps, and actionable priorities.
