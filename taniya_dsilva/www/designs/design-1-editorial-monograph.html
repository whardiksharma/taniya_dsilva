I. Executive Thesis

Taniya D’silva is a strategy and programme leader with 15 years of experience driving organisational strategy, multi-stakeholder programmes, executive communications, and donor and government partnerships across climate, gender equity, labour markets and public health in LMIC contexts.

She works at the intersection of research, strategy and implementation, helping organisations translate evidence and strategic priorities into practical programmes, partnerships and organisational action. Her experience spans advisory work with nonprofits, foundations, private sector, government bodies and other social-impact organisations.

Her approach combines strategic thinking with practical execution: understanding the evidence, clarifying the strategic choices, aligning stakeholders and translating priorities into implementable programmes and systems.
