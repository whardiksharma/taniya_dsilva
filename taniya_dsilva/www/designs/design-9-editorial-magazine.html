The Architecture of Enduring Social Transformation

How fifteen years navigating complex LMIC governance, planetary boundaries, and multilateral consortia shaped a rigorous new standard for advisory leadership.

By Taniya D’silva, PMP® · Wise Impact Consulting Solutions · New Delhi & Regional Desks
Taniya D'silva in strategic consultation. With 15+ years across multilateral portfolios, she advises executive leadership on policy execution.

Cross-sector social transformation is rarely halted by a lack of capital or noble intent. Rather, systemic programmes falter at the fragile seams between macro-policy rhetoric and frontline operational realities. Over the past fifteen years, across the Himalayan mountain watersheds to national manufacturing belts, our practice has focused on bridging this exact institutional void.

Through Wise Impact Consulting Solutions, Taniya D’silva provides boards, UN agencies, bilateral missions, and non-profit leaders with the rigorous analytical scaffolding required to convert complex philanthropic goals into durable institutional realities.

“True systemic resilience requires aligning high-level ministerial policy with the lived experience of frontline women workers and rural watershed stewards.”
— Taniya D’silva, Programme Director

Whether architecting multi-million dollar public health rollouts under PMP® disciplines or publishing peer-reviewed climate dispatches with French national outlets like L’Express, every engagement is anchored in unshakeable empirical integrity.
